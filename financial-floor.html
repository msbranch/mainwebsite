See what your money is doing.

The Financial Floor turns the bank statement you already have into a clear monthly picture of your income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and what remains after the month.

How It Works

One month. One clear picture.

1

Report your income

Tell us your actual take-home income for the month you want to look at.

2

Add your statements

Upload the bank and credit-card statements you choose to include. No bank login — ever.

3

See it sorted

Your spending is separated into fixed and variable — the bills that repeat and the ones that move.

4

Read your floor

You get your Net Income and your Net-Income Margin for the month, in plain numbers.

The Math

Simple on purpose.

Two calculations tell you where the month actually landed — nothing hidden, nothing you can't check yourself.

Net Income Actual Income − Fixed Expenses − Variable Expenses
Net-Income Margin ( Net Income ÷ Actual Income ) × 100
Your Result

Four ways a month can land.

In the Red

More went out than came in this month. The floor shows exactly where.

In the Black

You broke even or held a thin margin. Steady, but little room to build yet.

In the Green

Real margin remained after the month — money with a job still to be given.

Needs Review

The month isn't clear-cut. We flag what to look at before you decide.

From there, The Financial Floor points you toward the building block that fits where you are — Protection, Accumulation, or Distribution.
