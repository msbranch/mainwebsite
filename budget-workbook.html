What makes it different

What it handles that other templates don’t

The accounting other “budget templates” quietly get wrong — handled correctly, so your numbers actually mean something.

✓Budget ≠ bank balance

“$215 left for groceries” is your plan, not the cash in your account. It keeps the two separate so you never confuse them.

✓Credit cards, done right

Card purchases and the payments that cover them are tracked without double-counting a single dollar.

✓Transfers, savings & fees

Moving money between accounts, saving, and fees are each counted once — correctly.

✓Start mid-month

Jumping in on the 14th? It catches you up from the 1st so the month still adds up.

✓New month, carried forward

Each new month starts from your carried-forward plan instead of a blank page.

✓You stay in charge

The assistant proposes or records only what you approve. Every decision is yours.
