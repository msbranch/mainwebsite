The Question

When income changes, who carries the responsibility?

Insurance exists because responsibility does not disappear when life hits you.

People

Income often supports more than one person. Consider who relies on your care, consistency, or contribution today.

Work

A job, business, or role can carry responsibilities that do not automatically stop when one person is no longer there.

Future

Some responsibilities are connected to what comes next. What future responsibility or opportunity would you not want to leave unsupported?

Assets

What have you worked to build or keep—such as a home, business interest, or another asset with responsibilities attached to it?

The question is not simply what policy exists. It is what could be left carrying the weight.
