Why not build it yourself?

You could connect a spreadsheet to AI yourself. Here's what you'd still have to build.

An AI assistant connected to a blank spreadsheet doesn't know the rules of money. Left to guess, it will:

✕ Without the workflow

  • Count a credit-card purchase, then count the card payment again
  • Treat money moved to savings as spending, or a transfer as income
  • Confuse your budget total with what's actually in the bank
  • Guess bill due dates from when charges posted
  • Lose track when a new month starts

✓ With the workflow

  • Card purchases and payments counted once
  • Transfers and savings handled correctly
  • Budget and bank balance kept separate
  • Due dates confirmed, never guessed
  • Each month carries your plan forward

Questions

Common questions

Can you see my budget?

No. You work in your own private copy. Making a copy does not give me access to it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You log activity yourself in plain words. That's the point: you stay aware of every dollar.

Which AI assistants work?

Any assistant that can read and edit Google Sheets, such as ChatGPT with Google Drive connected or Claude with Google Sheets connected. Editing features depend on your plan and settings.

Does the AI decide things for me?

No. Your CFO records what you tell it and helps you think through your own numbers. You choose whether it shows you each entry first or records routine items directly, and you make every decision. It's an educational tool, not financial advice.

What if my assistant can't edit the sheet?

The setup guide's troubleshooting covers the common causes. You can also email info@morganbranch.co.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — through the Google Sheets app and your assistant's mobile app, if your plan supports editing.
