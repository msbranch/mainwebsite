Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what the Protection Assessment on this site collects, how that information is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Protection Assessment and the automated summary it prepares. It does not describe unrelated third‑party sites.

What we collect

When you complete the Protection Assessment and submit your result, we collect:

  • your first name, last name, and email address;
  • your selected age band and the answers you chose in the assessment (all from fixed options — the assessment does not collect free‑text about your finances);
  • your acknowledgment of the educational nature of the assessment, and your separate, optional marketing choice.

How your information is used

  • To prepare and show your preliminary protection direction — a general, educational starting point determined by your answers.
  • To prepare an automated written summary that helps explain that direction.
  • To follow up with you about a protection conversation you request, and to manage that relationship in Morgan Branch the Broker's private internal system ("Lead Desk").

Automated summary generation (Zapier and OpenAI)

To create the automated written summary, a minimized set of your assessment information is sent to our workflow provider (Zapier) and an AI model provider (OpenAI). Important limits on this step:

Your email address and last name are not sent to the automated summary‑generation step. Only your first name and selected assessment answers are used to write the summary.

The result is an automated, preliminary protection direction and educational summary. It is not a quote, a policy illustration, a coverage calculation, an underwriting decision, an eligibility decision, an approval, or a final recommendation. Any actual coverage is subject to insurer underwriting, policy terms, availability, and applicable law.

How your information is retained

Your assessment information and contact details are retained in the Lead Desk internal system so Morgan Branch the Broker can follow up and manage the relationship. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see “Your choices” below).

Marketing choices

Optional marketing email is separate and unchecked by default. You only receive that email if you affirmatively opt in, and you can opt out at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that operate this workflow (for example, our hosting, workflow automation, and AI‑summary providers) so they can perform those functions on our behalf, and where required by law.

Your choices

  • Access or deletion: you can request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your information.
  • Marketing: you can opt out of marketing email at any time.

To make a request, contact Morgan Branch the Broker at info@morganbranch.co.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@morganbranch.co.
