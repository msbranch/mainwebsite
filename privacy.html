Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026.

This policy explains what Morgan Branch the Broker collects, how that information is used, and the choices you have across the Protection Assessment, the secure Quote Overview, the optional Financial Floor add-on, and the payment and scheduling that support them. It does not describe unrelated third-party sites.

1. Protection Assessment

When you complete the Protection Assessment and submit your result, we collect your first name, last name, and email address; your selected age band and the answers you chose (all from fixed options — the assessment does not collect free-text about your finances); your acknowledgment of the educational nature of the assessment; and your separate, optional marketing choice.

We use this to prepare and show your preliminary protection direction — a general, educational starting point determined by your answers — and an automated written summary that helps explain it, and to follow up about a protection conversation you request.

To create the automated summary, our system sends a minimized set of your assessment information server-side to an AI model provider (OpenAI). The summary is generated by our own server-side process; it is not routed through a third-party workflow automation service.

Your email address and last name are not sent to the automated summary-generation step. Only your first name and selected assessment answers are used to write the summary.

The result is an automated, preliminary protection direction and educational summary. It is not a quote, policy illustration, coverage calculation, underwriting decision, eligibility decision, approval, or final recommendation. Any actual coverage is subject to the insurer's underwriting, policy terms, availability, and applicable law.

2. Quote Requests and Quote Overview

If you request a quote, we collect the details needed to prepare it and to deliver your secure Quote Overview — for example your name, email, the coverage you want to explore, and your confirmation of residency. A date of birth, if provided for a quote, is encrypted and is not shown back in the internal system. Your Quote Overview and any carrier document are delivered through a private, verified session; access requires the secure link and an emailed one-time code.

3. Financial Floor Add-On

If you add the optional Financial Floor money-clarity service, it may handle:

  • your name, email, appointment details, and linked quote/service records;
  • payment status and internal payment reference identifiers, but not your full card number or security code;
  • completed-month statements or figures you choose to share;
  • Financial Floor work product such as categorized expense information, fixed/variable organization, and the Monthly Money Snapshot, if retained;
  • essential security, session, rate-limit, and audit records.

4. Payments and Scheduling

The $67 Financial Floor payment is collected by our payment processor, Stripe, on Stripe's secure hosted page. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code; we keep only non-sensitive payment status and internal reference identifiers. Stripe's handling of your payment information is described in Stripe's privacy notice at stripe.com/privacy.

Scheduling (and self-service rescheduling from your confirmation email) uses your appointment details and the email connected to your Quote Overview.

How we use information

We use information to deliver the requested service, confirm payment and scheduling, provide support, maintain required business and accounting records, protect private services, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information. We do not place statement content, transaction detail, Financial Floor materials, or sensitive financial information in payment records, URLs, marketing systems, or advertising analytics.

5. Service Providers and Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that operate these services, so they can perform those functions on our behalf, and where required by law. These include:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure (Amazon Web Services) — to run the site and the private internal system;
  • Email delivery (Amazon Simple Email Service) — to send secure links, verification codes, and confirmations;
  • Payment processing (Stripe) — to collect the Financial Floor payment (see stripe.com/privacy);
  • Protection Assessment summary generation (OpenAI) — our server-side process sends the minimized information described in Section 1 to OpenAI to create the automated educational summary.

6. Security, Retention, and Deletion Requests

We use access controls and encryption designed to protect sensitive information. Access to Financial Floor materials is limited to Morgan and service providers that need it to operate the service.

Information is retained only as needed to provide the service and to maintain business, accounting, security, dispute, and legal records, and is then deleted or deidentified when appropriate. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing info@morganbranch.co. A deletion request may not remove information that must be retained for the purposes above, or information still held in routine backups for their scheduled lifecycle.

Optional marketing email is separate and unchecked by default; you only receive it if you affirmatively opt in, and you can opt out at any time.

7. Essential Cookies and Private Sessions

We use essential cookies or similar technologies to secure your private Quote Overview, payment, and scheduling sessions — for example to keep a verified session active and to protect these flows. These are required for the secure services to work and are not used for advertising.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your information, can be sent to info@morganbranch.co.

This page describes current practices and may be updated as the service changes. Insurance services are offered where licensed. Information here is educational and does not constitute specific tax, legal, or insurance advice.
